The foundation of working with location data. This path takes someone with no GIS
background through how spatial data is stored, why every flat map distorts the
world, where to get real open data, and the analysis moves that answer questions
a spreadsheet cannot. Finish it and the programming and web-mapping paths will
feel like putting familiar ideas into code.
