Every whole number greater than 1 breaks down into a product of prime numbers,
and that breakdown is unique. For example, 60 is 2 times 2 times 3 times 5, so
its prime factors are 2, 3, and 5, and the largest is 5.

Your job is to find the **largest prime factor** of this number:

```
600851475143
```

Trial division from the smallest factor upward is enough. Start with the divisor
2. While the current divisor divides the number evenly, divide it out. When it no
longer divides evenly, move to the next divisor and repeat. Because you remove
each factor completely before moving on, every divisor you successfully pull out
is prime. You only need to keep going while the divisor squared is less than or
equal to the value that remains. Whatever value is left above 1 at the end is the
largest prime factor.
