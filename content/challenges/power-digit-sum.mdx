## Power Digit Sum

The number 2 raised to the power 15 is 32768, and the sum of its digits is
3 + 2 + 7 + 6 + 8 = 26.

Your job is the same operation at a much larger scale.

Compute 2 raised to the power 1000 (that is, 2^1000), then add up all of its
decimal digits.

Python integers are arbitrary precision, so `2 ** 1000` gives the exact value
with no rounding. Do not use floating point or scientific notation, those would
throw away the low digits you need.

### What to submit

Run your code, read the printed total, and submit that single number: the sum
of the decimal digits of 2^1000.
