## Digit Factorials

The number `145` has a curious property: the sum of the factorials of its digits
equals the number itself.

```
145 = 1! + 4! + 5! = 1 + 24 + 120 = 145
```

A few numbers do this. Your job is to find all of them and add them up.

### The task

1. For a candidate number `n`, compute the sum of the factorial of each of its
   digits. For `145` that is `1! + 4! + 5! = 145`.
2. Keep `n` only if that sum is exactly equal to `n`.
3. Exclude `1` and `2`. They trivially equal `1!` and `2!`, but a single digit is
   not a sum in this sense, so they do not count.
4. Add together every number that survives, and submit that total.

### Why the search terminates

You cannot scan forever, but you do not need to. The largest single-digit
factorial is `9! = 362880`. A number with `d` digits can therefore have a digit
factorial sum of at most `d * 362880`. Once `d * 362880` has fewer than `d`
digits, no `d`-digit number can possibly reach its own digit factorial sum, so
the search has a provable ceiling. Checking up to seven digits is more than
enough.

Run your code, read the total, and submit that single number.
