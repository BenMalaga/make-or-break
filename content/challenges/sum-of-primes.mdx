There are 25 prime numbers below 100. This challenge asks for their sum.

Loop over every number from 2 up to 99, test each one for primality, and add the
primes together. Print the total, then submit it.

It is a small enough range that you can even sanity-check the first few by hand:
2 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 11 is 28, and it climbs from there.
