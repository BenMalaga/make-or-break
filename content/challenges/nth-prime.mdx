The prime numbers start 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, and never stop. They thin out as they
grow, but they always keep coming.

Your job is simple to state: find the **1000th** prime number. Counting from the
first prime (2), keep listing primes until you reach the thousandth one, and that
value is the answer.

Writing the check by hand is the point. A number is prime when nothing between 2
and its square root divides it evenly. Loop upward, test each candidate, and keep
a running count of how many primes you have seen. When the count reaches 1000, the
current number is your answer.
