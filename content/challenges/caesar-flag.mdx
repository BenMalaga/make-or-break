A Caesar cipher hides a message by sliding every letter a fixed number of steps
through the alphabet. Shift by 3 and A becomes D. Because there are only 25
possible shifts, it offers no real secrecy: you can simply try them all.

This flag was rotated by 13, the shift known as ROT13:

```
ZBO{pnrfne_fgvyy_snyyf}
```

Shift each letter back 13 places (or forward 13, since ROT13 is its own inverse)
and the flag appears. Only the letters move; the braces and underscores stay
where they are. Recover it and submit the `MOB{...}` flag.
