## About this target

Juice Shop is the most popular deliberately insecure web application in the
world, maintained by OWASP. It is a real Angular and Node application with a
SQLite backend, seeded with dozens of vulnerabilities that map to the OWASP Top
10. It is the ideal place to build web instincts.

Your two objectives here: **find the hidden Score Board** (user), then **log in
as the administrator** by abusing the login query (root).

<Callout type="security" title="Authorized target only">
You are running this container on your own machine. That is the only reason it
is legal to attack it. Never point these techniques at an application you do not
own or have written permission to test.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="How flags work on Phase A machines">
Docker and bring-your-own-VM boxes use proof tokens revealed through the hints
once you have genuinely completed the objective. Capture a flag by doing the
work, then submitting the token. Phase B hosted machines will issue real
per-instance flags planted on the target.
</Callout>

Spin the container up, confirm the shop loads in your browser, then work the
