## About this target

Kioptrix Level 1 is where a huge number of people cut their teeth. It is a small
Linux VM running a handful of services that were already old when the box was
built, which makes it a clean lesson in the loop that never changes: enumerate
everything, read the version numbers, match one to a public exploit, and use it.
No trick puzzles, just discipline and attention to detail.

Your two objectives here: **get a foothold and read the user flag** (user), then
**land a root shell and read the root flag** (root).

<Callout type="security" title="Authorized target only">
This VM runs entirely on your own hypervisor, on an isolated lab network. That
is what makes attacking it legal. Keep it off any network you do not control.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Verify the download">
VulnHub mirrors change over time. Open the entry linked in the setup panel, grab

import.
</Callout>

## The loop

1. Find the target on your network.
2. Map every open port and identify each service and version.
3. Turn the weakest, oldest service into a shell (user).
4. Confirm your privilege level and escalate to root (root).

Work it yourself first. The hints are here when you are genuinely stuck, and each
one costs points, so earn them.
