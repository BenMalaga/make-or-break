## Recon

Load `http://localhost:3000` and click around. Note the SPA never reloads the
page, which means routing happens client-side in JavaScript.

## Finding the Score Board (user)

1. Open dev tools, Network tab, and reload. Find `main.js` (the Angular bundle).
2. Search the bundle for `score-board`. You will find the route registered even
   though nothing links to it.
3. Browse to `http://localhost:3000/#/score-board`.

The Score Board is the map of every challenge in the app. Objective complete.
Proof token: `MOB{score_board_is_the_map}`.

## Admin access (root)

The login endpoint builds a SQL query from your email input without proper
parameterization.

1. Go to the login page.
2. Enter email `admin'--` (note the trailing space after the two dashes) and any
   password.
3. The injected comment truncates the password check, and you authenticate as
   the first user in the table, the administrator.

You are now admin. Proof token: `MOB{admin_is_just_a_query_away}`.

## Lessons learned

- Client-side routing does not hide anything. The whole app ships to the browser.
- String-concatenated SQL with user input is the oldest and still one of the most
  common critical bugs. Parameterized queries kill this entire class.
