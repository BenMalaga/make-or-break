## About this target

DC-1 is a small Linux VM built to teach the loop that never changes. It puts a
dated content management system out front, and everything flows from there:
enumerate the box, fingerprint the CMS, match its version to a public exploit,
get a shell, then escalate. No trick puzzles, just recon and attention to
version numbers.

Your two objectives here: **get a foothold through the CMS and read the user
flag** (user), then **escalate to a root shell and read the root flag** (root).

<Callout type="security" title="Authorized target only">
This VM runs entirely on your own hypervisor, on an isolated lab network. That
is what makes attacking it legal. Keep it off any network you do not control,
and never point these techniques at a system you do not own.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="How flags work on Phase A machines">
Bring-your-own-VM boxes use proof tokens revealed through the hints once you
have genuinely completed the objective. Do the work on the target, then submit
the token. Phase B hosted machines will issue real per-instance flags planted
on the box.
</Callout>

## Setup

VulnHub mirrors change over time. Open the entry linked in the setup panel, grab
the current download, and verify the checksum if one is published before you
import. Boot the VM on your lab network, then find it from your attacker box.

## The loop

1. Find the target on your network.
2. Map every open port and identify each service and version.
3. Turn the outdated CMS into a shell (user).
4. Enumerate the box from the inside and escalate to root (root).

Work it yourself first. The hints are here when you are genuinely stuck, and each
one costs points, so earn them.
